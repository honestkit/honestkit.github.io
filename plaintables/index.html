PlainTables

Copy any web table to Excel, Google Sheets, CSV or Markdown in one click. No row limits, no account, no tracking.

What it does

  • Click the icon to see every table on the page (hover to highlight it)
  • Copy for Excel / Google Sheets (paste keeps rows and columns), download CSV or a real .xlsx, or copy Markdown
  • Handles merged cells (colspan/rowspan) and modern div-based data grids (ARIA grids); skips hidden and layout tables
  • Keeps numbers as numbers in XLSX (1,234.50 / $9 / 12% become real numbers)
  • CSV export guards against spreadsheet formula injection
  • Reads only the tab you click it on (activeTab); nothing leaves your browser

Frequently asked questions

Is PlainTables a Table Capture alternative?

Yes, it does the same core job: export HTML tables from the current page to spreadsheet formats. At the time of our research Table Capture's free tier had a row cap (around 250 rows) with a yearly Pro plan; PlainTables' free version has no row limit.

Can I paste into Google Sheets or Excel?

Yes. “Copy for Excel/Sheets” copies tab-separated data, so rows and columns land in the right cells. You can also download CSV or XLSX.

Does it handle merged cells?

Yes. colspan and rowspan are expanded into a rectangular grid, and ARIA grid layouts built from divs are detected too.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The extraction runs in your tab and the conversion runs in the popup. PlainTables makes no network requests.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes — paste a table's HTML into the free HTML table to CSV converter on this site.
