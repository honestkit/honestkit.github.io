    Frequently asked questions

    Can I get ALL DNS records of a domain?

    Not from public DNS: the ANY query is deprecated and zone transfers are disabled almost everywhere. This tool queries each common type (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, NS, TXT, CAA, SOA) plus _dmarc, which covers what most checks need. Subdomains have to be looked up one by one.

    How do I check if an email address is valid?

    This page checks the domain part: if it has no MX record (or a null MX), mail to it will bounce. It cannot tell whether the mailbox itself exists; no honest public check can without sending mail. For lists, the bulk Email Syntax & MX Checker below flags typos, disposable domains and dead domains.

    What makes a good SPF record?

    Exactly one TXT record starting with v=spf1, listing your mail services and ending in ~all or -all, with at most 10 DNS-lookup terms (include, a, mx, ptr, exists, redirect) including nested ones. +all lets anyone send as you.

    Which DMARC policy should I use?

    Start with p=none plus a rua= address to collect reports, fix every legitimate sender, then move to p=quarantine and p=reject. Gmail and Yahoo require at least p=none for bulk senders.

    Is anything sent to your server?

    No. The page has no analytics. The domain name goes directly from your browser to Cloudflare's resolver (cloudflare-dns.com); nothing else is sent anywhere.
